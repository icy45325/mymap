Lumi 隐私政策

生效日期：2026-06-27

Lumi 是一款纯本地的旅行足迹应用。我们的原则很简单：你的记忆属于你。我们不收集、不上传、不出售你的任何个人信息。

我们不收集任何数据

Lumi 没有账号、没有服务器。你的足迹、照片引用、心愿、个人资料全部只保存在你的设备本地（通过 Apple 的 SwiftData）。卸载 App 或在 App 内删除即可清除，无任何云端副本。

权限说明

照片

用于读取你相册照片的拍摄位置以自动归纳「去过的地方」，以及把你选择的照片作为足迹封面。照片不会离开你的设备，我们只引用相册标识，不拷贝、不上传原图。

位置

用于在地图上定位你的当前位置以便点亮足迹。我们不做后台持续追踪，不上传你的位置。

相机

仅用于扫描朋友明信片的二维码。不录制、不上传任何影像。

使用到的 Apple 服务

地图显示、地点搜索与反向地理编码经由 Apple 地图处理；内购经由 App Store 处理。这些数据由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理，我们不接收、不存储。Lumi 不集成任何第三方分析或广告 SDK。

诊断日志

App 仅使用系统本地日志（OSLog）做自我诊断，永不离开设备，不上报任何后端。

明信片分享

你主动分享的明信片（口令 / 二维码 / 链接 / 隔空投送）只包含你选择放入的那条足迹信息，点对点传输，无我们的服务器中转。

儿童

Lumi 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集其信息。

内购

Lumi Plus 的订阅与买断通过 Apple 处理，我们不接触你的支付信息。

政策变更

如本政策有更新，我们会更新本页顶部的生效日期。

联系我们

有任何隐私问题，请联系：icywang45325@gmail.com（个人开发者）。

Lumi Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-06-27

Lumi is a fully on-device travel-footprint app. Our principle is simple: your memories are yours. We do not collect, upload, or sell any of your personal information.

We collect no data

Lumi has no account and no server. Your footprints, photo references, wishes, and profile are stored only locally on your device (via Apple's SwiftData). Deleting items in-app or uninstalling removes them; there is no cloud copy.

Permissions

Photos

Used to read the location of your photos to suggest places you've been, and to use photos you pick as footprint covers. Photos never leave your device; we reference library identifiers only and never copy or upload originals.

Location

Used to place your current position on the map when lighting a footprint. We do no background tracking and never upload your location.

Camera

Used only to scan a friend's postcard QR code. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Apple services we rely on

Map display, place search, and reverse geocoding are handled by Apple Maps; purchases are handled by the App Store. That data is processed by Apple under its privacy policy; we neither receive nor store it. Lumi integrates no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Diagnostics

The app uses only the system's local logging (OSLog) for self-diagnostics. It never leaves your device and is not sent to any backend.

Postcard sharing

Postcards you choose to share (code / QR / link / AirDrop) contain only the single footprint you put in, transmitted peer-to-peer with no server of ours in between.

Children

Lumi is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

In-app purchases

Lumi Plus subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple; we never see your payment information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.

ساري المفعول: 2026-06-27

لا يوجد في Lumi حساب ولا خادم. تُخزَّن بصماتك ومراجع صورك وأمنياتك وملفك الشخصي محليًا على جهازك فقط. حذفها داخل التطبيق أو إزالة التطبيق يمحوها؛ لا توجد نسخة سحابية.
