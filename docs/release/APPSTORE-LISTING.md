## 🇬🇧 English (en)

- **Name**: Lumi: Travel Footprints
- **Subtitle**: Light the places you've been
- **Keywords**: travel,footprint,map,countries visited,passport,postcard,badges,trip log,world map,places,memories,wishlist,cities

**Promotional Text**
> Light up everywhere you've been into your own star map. Recover footprints from your photos and mail them to friends as postcards.

**Description**
```
Lumi turns the world you've walked into your own star map.

Every place you've been, light it up on the map — it becomes a star on your timeline, a badge on your wall, a stamp in your passport. Not silent background tracking, but a deliberate, ceremonial keepsake you light by hand.

Too many to add one by one? Recover them from your photos. Lumi reads the location in your photos and groups them into footprints by city and country — you just pick. Your photos always stay on your device.

Turn a trip into a postcard and send it by QR, link, or AirDrop. Friends receive it right inside Lumi, into their own postcard wall.

KEY FEATURES
· Light up the map — tap countries / cities, record the year, watch your world progress
· Trails timeline — look back by year; see how many countries and cities you hit this year
· Recover from Photos — auto de-duplicated by location, fill in your history in a tap
· Achievement wall — unlock crafted 3D badges with an unlock celebration
· Home-screen widgets — Stats or Flags, switch anytime
· Postcards — image / QR / link / AirDrop; scan to receive
· Passport — colored by your nationality, filled with entry stamps
· Wishlist — places you want to go, popular spots in one tap

PRIVACY FIRST
Lumi is fully on-device with no account. Your footprints, photos, and location stay on your device — we collect, upload, and sell nothing.

LUMI PLUS
Upgrade for watermark-free HD postcard export, with more perks coming. Monthly / yearly subscriptions and a lifetime option.
```
